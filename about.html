About Model Shift

Model Shift explains what AI product, pricing, and policy changes actually mean for the people using these tools day to day. Every AI outlet will tell you what happened. We try to answer the next question: so what should you actually do differently?

Research and analysis for this site are produced with AI assistance (Claude) under human editorial direction, drawing on primary sources and public reporting. We link to our sources in every piece and disclose affiliate relationships where they exist.
